This lets the Home tile inside Vetro's overlay open a chosen installed app, with a custom label of 1–12 characters. For example, the tile can open a preferred launcher while the physical Home button continues to toggle Vetro.

Samsung Home remains the default and can be restored in settings. The destination is validated before saving. If the saved app becomes unavailable, the generated tile falls back to Samsung Home while retaining the saved preference.

This PR configures the onscreen tile. Physical Home assignment is covered by #119, optional double press by #167, and direct physical interception by #30. It does not change power-on behavior or automatically return to an app after exit.

The branch is based directly on the 0.5.0-based upstream baseline `180d9845`. The Samsung Home launch correction in #169 is separate and is not included here.
